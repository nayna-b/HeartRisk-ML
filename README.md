# 🫀 Heart Disease Predictor

## Overview
The **Heart Disease Predictor** is a machine learning model that estimates the risk of cardiovascular disease based on patient inputs such as **age, blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose levels, and lifestyle factors**.

## How It Works
1. User enters their **health details**.
2. The model analyzes risk factors and predicts:
   - Whether the person **has a risk of heart disease**.
   - The **percentage chance of cardiovascular disease**.
